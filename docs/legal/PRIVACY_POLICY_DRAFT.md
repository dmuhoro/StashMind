# DRAFT: Privacy Policy for StashMind
*Notice: This document is an initial draft intended for formal legal review by qualified counsel prior to public launch.*

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Data:** Email address, display name, user-selected audience preset (Student, Learner, Professional), and timezone.
- **Source Inputs:** Text, documents, or URLs submitted for educational summarization and audio lesson generation. All uploaded source materials are private to your user account by default.
- **Learning & Practice Activity:** Insight cards saved, practice session turns and scores, spaced repetition review logs, and streaks.

## 2. How Information is Processed
- Source inputs are processed strictly to generate two-host audio lessons, grounded insight cards, and practice scenarios.
- Content hashing (SHA-256) is utilized to eliminate redundant AI calls without exposing raw private text in shared caches.
- We do not sell, rent, or monetize your uploaded documents or study data.

## 3. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & Kenya Data Protection Act)
Under applicable data protection laws, you retain the right to:
- Access and receive a copy of your personal data in structured JSON format.
- Request immediate, irreversible deletion of your account and all associated sources and cards.
- Withdraw consent at any time.

## 4. Age Restrictions
StashMind requires users to be at least 13 years old (or 16 years old in jurisdictions governed by the GDPR). We do not knowingly process data from minors below these thresholds.

## 5. Contact & Data Protection Officer
For inquiries or data deletion requests, contact: `privacy@stashmind.ai`.
